    03 / How ordering works

    Made to order · no payment on this site

    Three steps,one message.

    We're a student team, not a web store: we print in batches and confirm every order in a chat. No payment is taken on this site.

    1. 01

      Fill your bag

      Pick colours, sizes and quantities. Your bag is kept on this device.

    2. 02

      We copy your order and open a chat with @frc_team_8806 — paste it and send.

    3. 03

      Pay and pick up

      We confirm the total, how to pay, and pickup at school or delivery in Taiwan, in the chat.
