02 / Results

Track record · 2022–2026

From rookies
to the international stage.

Founded in 2021, we won the Regional in our very first season in 2022 — and every season since, we've taken 8806 further afield: Hawaii, Istanbul, Arizona and Shanghai.

At our peak, our overall ability ranked among the world's top 100 teams.

        the robot.

        • Designs every mechanismDesigns the robot and its mechanisms, from the swerve drivetrain to the intake, with engineering drawings in Onshape.
        • Makes the parts in-houseLaser cutter, CNC and large machine tools, with our own safety rules and training.
