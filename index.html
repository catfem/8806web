Drivetrain

Moves every direction.
All at once.

Four swerve modules — each with its own drive and steering motor — let the robot strafe, spin and sprint at the same time, all controlled by student-written Java.

  • Swerve modules ×4
  • Kraken brushless motors
  • Odometry

Elevator

Built to
reach higher.

A multi-stage elevator carries the scoring mechanism from the carpet to the top of the goal — designed in Onshape, then cut on our laser cutter and CNC.

  • Onshape CAD
  • Laser & CNC machined
  • Aluminium + carbon fibre

Intake

Touch it.
Own it.

Rows of compliant green wheels grab a game piece the instant they touch it, then hand it to the scoring mechanism.

  • Compliant-wheel rollers
  • Fold-away pivot
